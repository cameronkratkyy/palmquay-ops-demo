# Palmquay Properties: the new-owner team (fictional people, demo only)

*Sample records for a demo. Not real people.*

Palmquay's residential team takes on single-family homes, condos, and townhomes for individual owners. These are the people who bring a new owner on. Lena Ortiz and Tom Becker also appear in the rest of the demo.

| Role | Name | Decides | Email (reserved demo domain) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner Onboarding Coordinator | Talia Brooks | Sends every follow-up to owners; enters each property in the software after the sign-offs | talia.brooks@palmquay.example |
| Broker of Record | Gail Mercer | Signs each management agreement for Palmquay (management accepted); answers for the trust account under Florida law | gail.mercer@palmquay.example |
| Trust Accountant | Owen Pratt | Accounting approved: where owner payouts go (confirmed by calling the owner at a number Palmquay already had), tenant deposits received, the owner ledger | owen.pratt@palmquay.example |
| Property Manager | Lena Ortiz | Leasing-ready or takeover complete: keys, association rules, the lead-paint form, the tenant already living there | lena.ortiz@palmquay.example |
| Controller | Tom Becker | A foreign owner's tax treatment, with the owner's own tax adviser | tom.becker@palmquay.example |

`.example` is a reserved domain that can't receive mail, so nothing in the demo can reach a real inbox.
