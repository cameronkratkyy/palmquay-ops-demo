What isn't counted, on either side
Waiting: days spent waiting on a contractor, a supplier, or a signer.
Getting the records into the folder: scans, exports, and attachments.
Second and third reminders when someone doesn't reply.
Rework when a problem slips through by hand, such as a payment on a wrong invoice or a contractor approved with lapsed insurance.
Status questions and meetings about where things stand.
How the counts are checked: tests/check_phase3f.py recounts every one from the raw records its own way (the locked answer keys, the documents, the plan, the quotes, the tracking, the invoices), checks that each minute is labeled as an assumption, and checks that this page's own math matches. Its file states the counting rule for each step. The minutes live in dashboard/by-hand.json.
